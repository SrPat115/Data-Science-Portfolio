# Blog 1 - Why Data Science Contexts Matter.

1) The main reason we should model social topics and people is to bring communities together. By considering people's interests, we can bring people with similar interests together. This can also help people get into different communities, creating a more diverse and thriving community. For example, the drawing community. People who like to draw are more likely to interact with painting or sketching photos/videos. By tracking their engagement, we can help further their interests and boost the morale of the community through likes and supportive comments. 
2) Using analytics of people's interests can cause ethical questions to arise. Questions such as, "Is it right to use extra information to engage people for longer?" Sure, knowing someone's age, gender, and ethnic background can be useful to  immerse them further in their interests, but do we really NEED that information? A major problem with social media platforms is that their algorithms are good... TOO good! These algorithms can include the traits I mentioned earlier, and even log metrics such as the times people log on. If that data is tracked, algorithms can become much stronger in keeping people engaged, and that is done on purpose to have people stay longer on apps/websites to see ads more.
